Difference detection prototype

Throwaway prototype for the ticket "Which Difference detection holds up on the synthetic pair?". Generated .

Question: which way of detecting Differences holds up on the synthetic pair, and what shape does a Difference have? Two methods run on both sample pairs, with the exact Offset and with a by-eye Offset that is mm off:
  • Objects match every drawn line, area, hatch and letter in the Old Plan with the same thing in the New Plan, moved by the Offset. Anything left over is a Difference, outlined as the whole object; changed letters widen to the whole word or the whole label. "Auto-refined" first corrects the by-eye Offset by voting.
  • Raster renders both Bodies at 4 px/mm, one on top of the other, and marks ink that has no ink in the other Plan within the tolerance (±0.25 or ±0.5 mm). Differences are pixel regions, outlined by their box.

Things to look at

  • Long pair, Raster, exact Offset: the false alarms sit on the 7 dashed station lines (control C1). Their dashes start at each Body's top, so after the Offset they are out of phase.
  • D5 / D6 under Raster: missed. The labels sit on a hatched area, and the tolerance around the hatch lines swallows the changed letters. At 4 px/mm, "15" → "18" (R2) also hides inside the tolerance.
  • D4 (stripe extended): Objects mark the whole old stripe and the whole new stripe. Raster marks only the part that changed. Which one should the user see?
  • D5 / D6 text: compare "Objects, exact Offset" (changed word) with "…whole label" (the whole label, as the ground truth draws it).
  • By-eye Offset without refinement: Objects flags nearly every object (exact matching has no slack). Raster at ±0.25 mm explodes; at ±0.5 mm it is quiet but misses more. With auto-refinement, Objects is back to exact.
